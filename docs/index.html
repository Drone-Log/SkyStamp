Features

From raw flight to finished video

Everything happens on your computer. Your footage never leaves it.

Reads DJI SRT files

Finds the SRT next to your video automatically. Supports both the new format (Neo, Mini 3/4, Air 3, Avata) and the classic one (Mini 2, Air 2).

Flight-path mini-map

Your route drawn live over a street map or satellite imagery, with the takeoff point and the drone's current position.

Satellites, battery & real speed

GPS lock and the speed measured by the drone itself, read from the MP4. Add your DJI Fly flight log to also show the satellite count and battery level.

Fast 4K rendering

Uses your GPU encoder (Apple, NVIDIA, Intel, AMD) when available, with automatic software fallback. Keeps resolution, frame rate and audio.

Overlay for editors

Export a transparent ProRes 4444 overlay to place on top of your footage in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere or Final Cut.

Open source

MIT licensed and on GitHub. There's a command-line version for batch jobs, and styles are small files you can share.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

Drop your video

Drag the .MP4 from your drone onto the window. The matching .SRT is picked up automatically.

Pick a style

Choose a look and the data you want, then scrub the timeline to preview any frame.

Create video

Choose where to save it and click once. Your finished video keeps the original quality.

FAQ

Questions pilots ask

How do I add the SRT flight data to my DJI video?

Turn on Video Subtitles in the DJI Fly camera settings before you record. The drone then saves a .SRT file next to every .MP4. Open the video in SkyStamp, which picks up the SRT automatically, choose a style and click Create video.

Which DJI drones does it work with?

Any DJI drone that records an SRT file: the newer bracket format (Neo, Neo 2, Mini 3 and 4 Pro, Air 3, Avata 2…) and the older one (Mini 2, Air 2…). It is tested most on the DJI Neo 2. If your model shows wrong data, please open an issue with a short sample SRT.

My drone didn't create an SRT file. What now?

The SRT is only written while Video Subtitles is switched on in DJI Fly, and it can't be recreated afterwards. Turn it on for your next flights. Without an SRT, SkyStamp has no flight data to show.

Is SkyStamp really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence, with no ads, no account and no paid tier. You can also change the code and share your version.

Can I put the overlay on my video in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere or Final Cut?

Yes. Choose MOV · ProRes 4444 overlay only as the output format. You get a transparent video with just the data, which you place on a track above your footage.

Can it show the number of GPS satellites and the battery level?

Yes, when you add the flight log from the DJI Fly app (FlightRecord_….txt). DJI encrypts these logs, so you need a free API key from developer.dji.com. Without a log, the overlay still shows GPS signal on/off.

Can I design my own overlay?

Yes. Click Customize…, then drag elements on the preview, choose what each one shows, and save it as your own style. Styles are small files you can export and share.

Does SkyStamp upload my videos?

No. Everything is processed on your computer. The only optional connections are map tiles for the mini-map and, if you add a flight log, DJI's key service to decrypt it.

Privacy & licence

Your flights stay yours

SkyStamp has no servers, no accounts and no tracking. It's free and open source: use it as it is, or change it however you like.

Everything stays on your computer

Your videos, SRT files and flight logs are processed locally. Nothing is uploaded, and finished videos are saved only where you choose.

No data collection

No analytics, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no account. SkyStamp doesn't know who you are or what you render.

Yours to use and modify

MIT licensed: use it, change it, share it, build on it. The full source code is on GitHub.

The only connections SkyStamp makes, both optional:
  • Map tiles for the mini-map are downloaded from OpenStreetMap or Esri. They see the map area requested, like any map website. Turn it off with Map background → None.
  • DJI Fly flight logs are encrypted by DJI. When you add one, SkyStamp asks DJI's key service, with your own DJI API key, for the keys to decrypt that log. This happens once per log. Without a flight log, nothing is sent to DJI.
